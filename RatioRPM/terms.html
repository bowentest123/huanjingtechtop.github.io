3. Calculations Are Reference Values

The App builds a full gear matrix from the wheel diameter, front chainrings, and rear cassette you enter, computes a gear ratio and development (rollout) for each chainring-and-cog combination, and converts your cadence into a theoretical speed. It also highlights the highest and lowest gears and the closest matches between gears.

These figures are mathematical estimates based on the nominal values you select. They do not account for tyre size and pressure, tread, rider weight, wheel and tyre deformation under load, drivetrain losses, tyre slip, terrain, gradient, wind, drafting, or the accuracy of your cadence input. Speeds shown in the App will therefore differ from a bike computer, power meter, or GPS device, and from your actual speed on the road or trail. Gear ratios and development are properties of your drivetrain, not predictions of performance.

Use the App before you ride, not while riding. The App is a planning and comparison tool, not a navigation, safety, training, or medical device. Do not operate the App, change your setup, or read the screen while cycling, and never let its output replace your own judgement, observation of traffic and trail conditions, or compliance with traffic laws. Choose gears that are safe and appropriate for the conditions and for your own fitness and equipment.

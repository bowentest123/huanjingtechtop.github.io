Privacy Policy — RatioRPM

RatioRPM does its gear math on your device. This page explains exactly what the App stores and what it never collects.

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Beijing Huanjing Technology Co., Ltd. ("Huanjing", "we", "us") built RatioRPM (the "App") as a fast, focused gear calculator for road, gravel, and MTB cyclists. Pick your wheel size, front chainrings, and rear cassette, then enter your cadence: the App computes the gear ratio and development (rollout) for every chainring-and-cog combination and converts cadence into speed. Those calculations are performed entirely on your device. This Privacy Policy applies to the App only; our website and other products are covered by our general Privacy Policy. The rules for using the App are set out in the RatioRPM Terms of Use, and help is available on the Support page.

1. At a Glance

  • No account, no sign-in, and no email address required.
  • No advertising, no analytics SDKs, no third-party trackers, and no cross-app tracking.
  • Your bike setup, cadence, and preferences stay on your device.
  • All ratios, developments, speeds, and highlights are computed locally; no connection is needed.
  • We operate no server for the App and hold no copy of your data.

2. Information the App Stores

Bike setup. The wheel diameter or wheel size you select, the front chainrings you choose, and the cog sizes in your rear cassette. These values describe a bicycle drivetrain, not a person, and are used only to build the gear matrix.

Calculation inputs and results. Your current cadence value (in the 30–130 rpm range) and the derived gear ratios, development values, speeds, and highlights shown on screen.

Preferences. Settings you choose inside the App, such as sound and haptic feedback preferences, so the App can restore your setup and behave consistently between launches.

This information is stored locally on your device using standard iOS app storage. The App does not upload it, does not maintain a user database, and does not require an internet connection for any of its gear calculations.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, calendar, precise or approximate location, advertising identifier, browsing history, health or fitness data, ride traces, or the content of other apps. The App does not know where you ride or how fast you actually travelled.

The App contains no code that transmits your setup or calculations to a server operated by us. It does not request permission for location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or push notifications. Haptic feedback and the subtle chain-click sound are produced locally through iOS system APIs; they do not record audio or use the microphone.

4. How We Use Information

The information described above is used only to:

  • Build your setup from the wheel size, chainrings, and cassette you select;
  • Compute the full gear matrix with a ratio and development for each combination;
  • Convert your cadence into speeds and update them live as you slide;
  • Highlight the highest and lowest gears and the closest matches between gears;
  • Play haptic feedback and the chain-click sound when you make an adjustment;
  • Restore your setup and preferences when you reopen the App.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or automated decision-making, and we do not sell or rent it.

5. Sharing and Third Parties

The App contains no third-party SDKs, advertising networks, or analytics services, and we do not share your information with data brokers or other companies for their own purposes. The App is distributed through the Apple App Store, and Apple's own store and device services are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. If you have opted in to sharing diagnostics with app developers in your iOS settings, Apple may make aggregate, non-identifying crash or usage information available to developers; we do not embed any analytics SDK of our own and do not receive your setup or calculations through that channel.

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect legal rights, but because the App has no account system and no server-side copy of your data, there is no user database for us to disclose from.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local data. Your setup, cadence, and preferences remain on your device until you change them or delete the App. Deleting the App removes its locally stored data.
  • No server copy. We do not keep a backup of your setup and cannot restore it for you. If the App offers an in-app reset, it clears the stored values on your device.
  • Support messages. If you email us, we use that message only to answer your question and retain it only as long as needed for that purpose.

You may also email us to ask what categories of data the App stores or to request assistance with deletion.

7. Security

Data stored on your device is protected by iOS app sandboxing. We apply reasonable measures in the design of the App, but no method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the App requires no account and stores no data on our servers, a parent or guardian can remove all stored values simply by deleting the App from the device.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete personal data. Because the App has no account system and we hold no server-side copy of your data, the most direct way to exercise these rights is through your device as described in Section 6. You may also contact us using the details below.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App evolves. Material changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

How do hearts work?

Each level starts with 3 hearts. A heart is lost when you place a word card into the wrong category, or when you tap the red "T" (translate) button on a card a second time. If all hearts are lost, the level ends — you can replay the level or use a Revive Card to continue with full hearts.

What are Joker Cards?

Joker Cards let you move any top card onto a matching family during a level. You receive 3 Jokers free when you first launch the App, and you can earn more through the daily check-in. Packs are also available for purchase.

What are Revive Cards?

Revive Cards restore your hearts to full so you can continue the current level after running out. New users receive 1 free.

My progress disappeared after reinstalling.

Turn on Settings > iCloud Sync on both installs and sign in with the same Apple Account. Your save is backed up to your private iCloud database and will be restored automatically on next launch.

How do I change the languages?

Settings has three independent options: System language (App UI), Card language (the words on the cards), and Translation language (definitions shown when you tap "T"). Example sentences always follow the Card language.

I bought a pack but didn't receive the cards.

First make sure the purchase completed in the App Store. Then try Settings > Restore Purchases. If that does not fix it, email us with your order number (from Apple's receipt) and we will help.

I want to request a refund.

Refunds are handled by Apple: report a problem at reportaproblem.apple.com. We never receive your payment details and cannot approve or reverse a charge.

Does the App collect my data?

No. The App has no ads and no analytics. See the Privacy Policy for the full list of what is stored on your device and how optional iCloud Backup works.

Troubleshooting

Before you email us

A few quick checks solve most issues.

  • 1Update to the latest version of Word Solitaire Study! from the App Store, then close and reopen the App.
  • 2For missing card packs, confirm the purchase completed in the App Store, then try Settings > Restore Purchases.
  • 3For lost progress, enable iCloud Sync and sign in with the same Apple Account on both installs.
  • 4For billing and refunds, contact Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com — we cannot access or change payment records.
What to include in your message: your device model, iOS version, the App version you are using, the level you were playing, what you did, what you expected, what happened instead, and a screenshot if it helps. For purchase issues, include your order number from Apple's receipt. Because the App does not send data to us, we cannot look up your progress remotely — these details let us reproduce the issue.

Privacy Policy — Word Solitaire Study!

The word-solitaire vocabulary game keeps your progress on your device. This page explains what it handles and what it never collects.

Last updated: November 25, 2026

huanjingtech ("we", "us") built Word Solitaire Study! (the "App"), a word-solitaire vocabulary learning game for iOS. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App handles and what it does not. It applies to the App only; our website and other products are covered by our general Privacy Policy. For help with the App, see the Support page.

1. Overview

Word Solitaire Study! ("the App") is a word-solitaire vocabulary learning game for iOS published by huanjingtech ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the App handles and what it does not.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

The App does not require an account or registration. We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information to us or to any third party. Specifically, the App contains:

  • No advertising SDKs;
  • No third-party analytics or tracking;
  • No collection of names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, location, photos, browsing history, or identifiers for tracking.

3. Data Stored On Your Device

Game progress (levels, achievements, joker cards, revive cards), your saved wordbook, and language/audio settings are stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage (UserDefaults). This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly enable iCloud Backup (Section 4).

4. iCloud Backup (Optional)

The App offers an optional iCloud sync feature, which you can turn on or off in Settings. When enabled, a single save file containing the game data listed in Section 3 is stored in your private iCloud database via CloudKit.

  • This data is stored in your own iCloud account and is visible only to you and to this App on your devices.
  • We (the developer) cannot access your iCloud data. CloudKit private databases are not readable by the developer.
  • Apple's privacy policy applies to iCloud storage: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

5. Purchases

In-app purchases (card packs) are processed by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit). We receive no payment details; Apple's privacy policy covers transaction data. Purchase history is associated with your Apple Account, and the App keeps a local record of granted purchases to support the "Restore Purchases" feature. We do not operate a payment server and do not build a purchase profile.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is rated for all ages, collects no personal data, and displays no third-party advertising.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

You can delete all local data at any time by uninstalling the App. If iCloud backup was enabled, you can remove the cloud save via the App's Settings (disabling iCloud Sync) or by managing App data in your iOS iCloud settings. You may also email us to ask what categories of data the App stores or to request assistance with deletion.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this document and provide notice in the App when required by law.

FAQ
---

Q: How do hearts work?
A: Each level starts with 3 hearts. A heart is lost when you place a word card
   into the wrong category, or when you tap the red "T" (translate) button on a
   card a second time. If all hearts are lost, the level ends — you can replay
   the level or use a Revive Card to continue with full hearts.

Q: What are Joker Cards?
A: Joker Cards let you move any top card onto a matching family during a level.
   You receive 3 Jokers free when you first launch the App, and you can earn
   more through the daily check-in. Packs are also available for purchase.

Q: What are Revive Cards?
A: Revive Cards restore your hearts to full so you can continue the current
   level after running out. New users receive 1 free.

Q: My progress disappeared after reinstalling.
A: Turn on Settings > iCloud Sync on both installs and sign in with the same
   Apple Account. Your save is backed up to your private iCloud database and
   will be restored automatically on next launch.

Q: How do I change the languages?
A: Settings has three independent options: System language (App UI), Card
   language (the words on the cards), and Translation language (definitions
   shown when you tap "T"). Example sentences always follow the Card language.

Q: I bought a pack but didn't receive the cards.
A: First make sure the purchase completed in the App Store. Then try
   Settings > Restore Purchases. If that does not fix it, email us with your
   order number (from Apple's receipt) and we will help.

Q: I want to request a refund.
A: Refunds are handled by Apple: report a problem at
   https://reportaproblem.apple.com

Q: Does the App collect my data?
A: No. The App has no ads and no analytics. See our Privacy Policy:
   https://huanjingtech.top/wordsolitairestudy/privacy_policy.txt

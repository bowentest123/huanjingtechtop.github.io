Privacy Policy for Word Solitaire Study!
=========================================

Last updated: November 25, 2026

1. Overview
-----------
Word Solitaire Study! ("the App") is a word-solitaire vocabulary learning game
for iOS published by huanjingtech ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data
the App handles and what it does not.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect
-------------------------
The App does not require an account or registration. We do not collect,
transmit, or sell any personal information to us or to any third party.
Specifically, the App contains:
- No advertising SDKs
- No third-party analytics or tracking
- No collection of names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts,
  location, photos, browsing history, or identifiers for tracking

3. Data Stored On Your Device
-----------------------------
Game progress (levels, achievements, joker cards, revive cards), your saved
wordbook, and language/audio settings are stored locally on your device using
standard iOS storage (UserDefaults). This data never leaves your device unless
you explicitly enable iCloud Backup (see below).

4. iCloud Backup (Optional)
---------------------------
The App offers an optional iCloud sync feature, which you can turn on or off
in Settings. When enabled, a single save file containing the game data listed
in section 3 is stored in YOUR private iCloud database via CloudKit.
- This data is stored in your own iCloud account and is visible only to you
  and to this App on your devices.
- We (the developer) cannot access your iCloud data. CloudKit private
  databases are not readable by the developer.
- Apple's privacy policy applies to iCloud storage:
  https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

5. Purchases
------------
In-app purchases (card packs) are processed by Apple through the App Store
(StoreKit). We receive no payment details; Apple's privacy policy covers
transaction data. Purchase history is associated with your Apple Account, and
the App keeps a local record of granted purchases to support the "Restore
Purchases" feature.

6. Children's Privacy
---------------------
The App is rated for all ages, collects no personal data, and displays no
third-party advertising.

7. Data Retention & Deletion
----------------------------
You can delete all local data at any time by uninstalling the App. If iCloud
backup was enabled, you can remove the cloud save via the App's Settings
(disabling iCloud Sync) or by managing App data in your iOS iCloud settings.

8. Changes to This Policy
-------------------------
If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this document
and provide notice in the App when required by law.

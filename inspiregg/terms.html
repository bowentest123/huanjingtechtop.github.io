3. Free Use and the One-Time Purchase

The App can be used free of charge. Free use lets you hatch up to three companions; after that, completed focus sessions still produce eggs, but hatching them requires premium access.

"Unlock Lifetime Premium" is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase. There is no subscription, no recurring billing, and no automatic renewal. Once the purchase is verified, premium access lets you hatch every egg and permanently unlocks the available chick costumes. The price is shown in the App and is charged to your Apple ID by Apple through the App Store.

Your purchase is tied to your Apple ID, not to a Huanjing account. Use "Restore Purchases" in the App's Settings to ask Apple to re-verify a purchase made previously with the same Apple ID. Because we have no account system, we cannot transfer a purchase to a different Apple ID, restore a purchase Apple cannot verify, or issue entitlement manually.

Privacy Policy — Inspiregg

Inspiregg works without an account. This page explains exactly what the App stores, what it syncs, and what it never collects.

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Beijing Huanjing Technology Co., Ltd. ("Huanjing", "we", "us") built Inspiregg (the "App") as a focus timer that rewards completed focus sessions with hatched companions. We designed the App so that your focus history stays on your device unless you choose to sync it. This Privacy Policy describes the limited information the App stores, how optional iCloud Sync works, and how purchases are handled. It applies to the App only; our website and other products are covered by our general Privacy Policy. The rules for using the App, including the one-time purchase and refunds, are set out in the Inspiregg Terms of Use.

1. At a Glance

  • No account, no sign-in, and no email address required.
  • No advertising, no analytics SDKs, no third-party trackers, and no cross-app tracking.
  • Focus history and preferences are stored locally on your device.
  • Optional iCloud Sync stores a copy of your collection in your own private iCloud database.
  • Purchases are processed entirely by Apple; we never receive your payment details.

2. Information the App Stores

Focus and collection data. The duration and date of each focus session, the companion or egg produced by that session (its identifier, name, picture index, and whether it has hatched), and derived statistics such as your total hatched count and total focus minutes.

Preferences. Your selected focus duration, the Deep Focus setting, the iCloud Sync setting, the interface language, and the state of the current focus session.

Purchase status. Whether the one-time "Unlock Lifetime Premium" entitlement has been verified with the App Store. The App reads a transaction verified by Apple; it does not receive your name, payment card details, billing address, or Apple ID.

This information is stored locally on your device using the standard iOS app preferences. All companion artwork is bundled inside the App itself; the App does not download or upload images.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, calendar, precise or approximate location, advertising identifier, browsing history, or the content of other apps. Deep Focus only observes whether the App itself moves into the background so a session can be ended; it does not monitor which apps you use or what you do in them.

The App contains no code that transmits your data to a server operated by us. We do not maintain a user database for the App, and we do not request permission for push notifications, location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.

4. How We Use Information

The information described above is used only to:

  • Run the focus timer, display your collection and achievements, and preserve your settings between launches;
  • Optionally sync your collection across your own devices through iCloud (Section 5);
  • Verify your one-time purchase entitlement (Section 6).

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or automated decision-making, and we do not sell or rent it.

5. iCloud Sync (Apple CloudKit)

iCloud Sync is enabled by default and can be turned off at any time in the App's Settings. When it is enabled, the App writes a single record containing your collection to the private database of your own iCloud account using Apple CloudKit. A private database is accessible only to the Apple ID that owns it; as the developer, we cannot read your private database.

When you enable sync, the App asks how to resolve any difference between the device and iCloud: Use Local replaces the iCloud copy with the data on this device, or Merge with iCloud combines both sides. Turning the switch off stops all syncing and keeps your data local; it does not delete a record already stored in your iCloud account.

If iCloud is unavailable, you are not signed in to iCloud, or your account is restricted, the App continues to work entirely offline with local data and will show a status message to that effect.

6. In-App Purchase

"Unlock Lifetime Premium" is a one-time, non-consumable purchase. There is no subscription and no automatic renewal. The transaction is handled by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit; payment is charged to your Apple ID and Apple's privacy policy governs that processing.

We do not operate a payment server, do not receive payment credentials, and do not build a purchase profile. The App checks Apple-verified transactions on the device to decide whether premium features are unlocked, and "Restore Purchases" asks Apple to re-verify purchases made previously with the same Apple ID.

7. Sharing and Third Parties

The App contains no third-party SDKs, advertising networks, or analytics services, and we do not share your information with data brokers or other companies for their own purposes. The only external services involved are Apple's own iCloud / CloudKit and App Store infrastructure, used solely for the features described in Sections 5 and 6 and governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect legal rights, but because the App has no account system and no server-side copy of your data, there is no user database for us to disclose from.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local data. Deleting the App from your device removes the locally stored focus history and preferences.
  • iCloud data. You control the synced record through your Apple ID. To remove it, delete the App's iCloud data in iOS Settings (Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Account Storage), or use "Use Local (Overwrite iCloud)" in the App to replace the stored record. Note that turning off iCloud Sync alone does not erase a record already stored in iCloud.
  • Purchases. Transaction records are retained by Apple in accordance with its own policies; we cannot delete them.

You may also email us to ask what categories of data the App stores or to request assistance with deletion.

9. Security

Data stored on your device is protected by iOS app sandboxing. When iCloud Sync is enabled, data in transit and at rest is protected by Apple's iCloud security. We apply reasonable measures in the design of the App, but no method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the App requires no account, a parent or guardian who believes a child has stored data through iCloud Sync can remove it using the steps in Section 8.

11. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete personal data. Because the App has no account system and we hold no server-side copy of your data, the most direct way to exercise these rights is through your device and your iCloud settings as described in Section 8. You may also contact us using the details below.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App evolves. Material changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Tap a question to expand the answer.

What is Inspiregg and do I need an account?

Inspiregg is a focus timer. Choose 5, 10, 15, 20, 30 or 60 minutes, stay with the session until the countdown ends, and the egg hatches into a companion you keep. There is no account, no sign-in and no email address required. The interface is currently in English and the App requires iOS 17.0 or later.

Why didn't my egg hatch?

An egg only hatches when the countdown reaches zero. Ending a session early, or switching to another app during a Deep Focus session, ends the attempt. Locking the screen does not end a session, so you can put the device down and let the timer run. In Normal mode, switching apps does not interrupt the timer.

What are the three focus modes?

Normal keeps the timer running even if you switch apps. Deep ends the session when you switch apps, but not when you lock the screen. Whitelist uses the iOS Screen Time and Family Controls picker to shield the apps you do not allow for that session.

Why can't some apps be shielded in Whitelist mode?

Shielding is performed by iOS through Screen Time, and iOS does not allow every system app to be shielded. Make sure you grant the requested Screen Time permission when asked. This is an operating-system limitation, not an App setting.

I don't see the Lock Screen countdown or the hatch notification.

Allow notifications when the App first asks, and keep Live Activities enabled in iOS Settings. The countdown is rendered by iOS as a Live Activity while the App is suspended, and a local notification with sound fires when the egg hatches. If you previously denied notification permission, re-enable it in iOS Settings > Notifications > Inspiregg.

Why did my companion's bond go down?

Bond gently decreases when a companion has not joined a focus session for a while, down to a minimum value; it never reaches zero. Completing a session together raises it again. Open the companion's card to see how long it has been idle and the current rule. The values refresh when you return to the App.

How do I sync my collection across devices?

Turn on iCloud Sync in Settings while signed in to iCloud on both devices. Your collection is stored in your own private iCloud database and is only readable by you. You can switch the setting off at any time. Without iCloud Sync, data stays on that device only.

Does the App work offline?

Yes. Focus sessions, hatching, the collection and the bond calculations all happen on your device and need no connection. You only need a connection for the one-time purchase, restoring a purchase and optional iCloud Sync.

Is there a subscription?

No. Inspiregg has a single non-consumable, one-time purchase called Unlock Lifetime Premium. The first three hatches are free; afterwards completed sessions are saved as eggs, and the purchase unlocks unlimited hatching, including eggs saved earlier. There are no recurring charges.

How do I restore my purchase after reinstalling or changing devices?

Open Settings and tap Restore Purchases, using the same Apple ID you purchased with. The App verifies the existing transaction with the App Store and unlocks the entitlement again. You are not charged a second time for a non-consumable purchase. If the App was freshly installed, it also checks your entitlement automatically on launch and when it returns to the foreground.

How do I request a refund?

All payments, billing questions and refunds are handled by Apple. We never receive your payment details and cannot approve or reverse a charge. Use reportaproblem.apple.com or contact Apple Support. The Inspiregg Terms of Use contain the same rule.

How do I delete all my data?

Settings includes a full data reset with a confirmation step. If iCloud Sync is on, the copy in your private iCloud database is removed as well. Deleting the App from the device also removes its local data. Your purchase entitlement is stored by the App Store and is not affected by a data reset.

What data do you collect from me?

None on our servers. Inspiregg has no account system, no analytics or advertising SDKs, and no code that uploads your focus history to us. Focus data and preferences stay on your device, plus an optional copy in your own private iCloud database. See the Privacy Policy for the full list.

Troubleshooting

Before you email us

A few quick checks solve most issues.

  • 1Update to the latest version of Inspiregg from the App Store, then close and reopen the App.
  • 2Check that notifications and Live Activities are allowed in iOS Settings.
  • 3For purchase questions, confirm you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase, then tap Restore Purchases.
  • 4For sync questions, confirm you are signed in to iCloud and that iCloud Sync is enabled on both devices.
  • 5For billing and refunds, contact Apple directly — we cannot access or change payment records.
What to include in your message: your device model, iOS version, the Inspiregg version you are using, what you did, what you expected, what happened instead, and a screenshot if it helps. Because the App does not send data to us, we cannot look up your sessions or collection remotely — these details let us reproduce the issue.

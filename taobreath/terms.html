3. Breathing Practice and Health Disclaimer

The App guides you through timed inhalations and voiced exhalations based on a traditional breathing practice. It is a general relaxation and mindfulness aid. It is not a medical device, not a diagnosis, and not medical, psychological, or professional advice, and it is not intended to treat, cure, or prevent any disease, symptom, or mental-health condition. The scene names describe common situations from a built-in catalogue; they are not an assessment of your health.

Practice gently and at your own pace. If you feel dizzy, short of breath, uncomfortable, or unwell, stop the session immediately and breathe normally. Do not hold your breath beyond what feels comfortable, and do not use the App while driving, operating machinery, bathing, or doing anything that requires your full attention. If you have a respiratory, cardiovascular, or other medical condition, are pregnant, or are under medical care, consult a qualified healthcare professional before starting any breathing exercise.

In an emergency — including severe distress, chest pain, or thoughts of harming yourself — contact your local emergency services or a qualified professional. The "Extreme Anger · Emergency" scene is a short self-calming exercise in the App, not an emergency or crisis service, and we do not monitor or respond to anything that happens inside the App.

6. Your Data and iCloud Sync

Your practice achievements and preferences are stored locally on your device using standard iOS app storage. iCloud Sync is enabled by default, can be turned off in Settings, and when enabled writes a single achievement record to the private database of your own iCloud account using Apple CloudKit. As the developer, we cannot read that private database.

When syncing, the App combines the local and iCloud records by keeping the higher value for each counter, so practice counts only stay the same or increase. There is no manual conflict-resolution choice. You are responsible for your device, your Apple ID, and your iCloud storage. We do not maintain a user database for the App, do not back up your history on any server of our own, and cannot recover or restore your data for you. Deleting the App removes the locally stored data; deleting the App's iCloud data in iOS Settings removes the synced record. Turning off iCloud Sync stops syncing but does not erase a record already stored in iCloud.

iCloud Sync depends on Apple's services, your iCloud sign-in, available storage, and network connectivity. We do not warrant that syncing will always be available or timely, and we are not liable for data lost through device loss, deletion, account changes, or failures of Apple's services.

Privacy Policy — Tao Breath

Tao Breath guides your breathing on your device. This page explains exactly what the App stores, how optional iCloud Sync works, and what it never collects.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Beijing Huanjing Technology Co., Ltd. ("Huanjing", "we", "us") built Tao Breath (the "App") as a guided breathing app based on the Six Healing Sounds (Liu Zi Jue), a traditional Taoist breathing practice. The App guides you through slow inhalations and six voiced exhalations — Xū, Hē, Hū, Sī, Chuī and Xī — across themed scenes for emotions, night, daily life and single-sound practice. We designed the App so that your practice history stays on your device unless you choose to sync it through your own iCloud account. This Privacy Policy applies to the App only; our website and other products are covered by our general Privacy Policy. The rules for using the App, including the one-time purchase and refunds, are set out in the Tao Breath Terms of Use, and help is available on the Support page.

1. At a Glance

  • No account, no sign-in, and no email address required.
  • No advertising, no analytics SDKs, no third-party trackers, and no cross-app tracking.
  • Practice history and preferences are stored locally on your device.
  • Optional iCloud Sync stores one achievement record in the private database of your own iCloud account.
  • Pronunciation audio is bundled inside the App; nothing is recorded or uploaded.
  • Purchases are processed entirely by Apple; we never receive your payment details.

2. Information the App Stores

Practice achievements. Your total number of completed sessions, total practice minutes, per-day counts of sessions and minutes, and how many times you completed each scene. These counters are derived from sessions you finish inside the App; the App does not read Apple Health data or any other app's data.

Preferences. Your chosen interface language (English or Simplified Chinese), the iCloud Sync switch, and a local cache of whether the one-time Lifetime Premium entitlement has been verified with the App Store.

Purchase status. Whether a non-consumable purchase has been verified with the App Store. The App reads an Apple-verified transaction; it does not receive your name, payment card details, billing address, or Apple ID.

This information is stored locally on your device using standard iOS app storage. The scene catalogue, the breathing choreography, and the six pronunciation audio files are bundled inside the App itself; the App does not download or upload media.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, calendar, precise or approximate location, advertising identifier, browsing history, health or fitness data, or the content of other apps. The App does not know why you chose a scene or how you felt during a session; the scene titles are part of a built-in catalogue, not information about you.

The App contains no code that transmits your data to a server operated by us, and we maintain no user database for it. It does not request permission for push notifications, location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or HealthKit. Pronunciation samples are played from bundled audio files, and the emergency anger guide uses the built-in iOS speech synthesizer; neither records audio nor uses the microphone.

4. How We Use Information

The information described above is used only to:

  • Run the guided breathing timer and show the current inhale / exhale phase and the sound to voice;
  • Display your achievements and daily practice history, and preserve your settings between launches;
  • Optionally sync the achievement record across your own devices through iCloud (Section 5);
  • Verify your one-time purchase entitlement (Section 6).

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or automated decision-making, and we do not sell or rent it.

5. iCloud Sync (Apple CloudKit)

iCloud Sync is enabled by default and can be turned off at any time in the App's Settings ("Sync Achievements via iCloud"). When it is enabled, the App writes a single achievement record to the private database of your own iCloud account using Apple CloudKit. A private database is accessible only to the Apple ID that owns it; as the developer, we cannot read your private database.

The App syncs when it launches and after each completed session. It reads the existing iCloud record, combines it with the local record by keeping the higher value for each counter, and writes the combined result back. Practice counters therefore only ever stay the same or increase; there is no manual "use local" or "merge" choice, and no record of individual breaths or individual sessions is transmitted.

Turning the switch off stops all syncing and keeps your data local. It does not delete a record already stored in your iCloud account (see Section 8). If you are not signed in to iCloud, your account is restricted, or the network is unavailable, the App continues to work entirely offline with local data.

6. In-App Purchase

"Lifetime Premium" is a one-time, non-consumable purchase that unlocks the scenes marked with a lock. There is no subscription and no automatic renewal. The transaction is handled by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit; payment is charged to your Apple ID and Apple's Privacy Policy governs that processing.

We do not operate a payment server, do not receive payment credentials, and do not build a purchase profile. The App checks Apple-verified transactions on the device to decide which scenes are unlocked, and "Restore Purchases" in Settings asks Apple to re-verify a purchase made previously with the same Apple ID.

7. Sharing and Third Parties

The App contains no third-party SDKs, advertising networks, or analytics services, and we do not share your information with data brokers or other companies for their own purposes. The only external services involved are Apple's iCloud / CloudKit and App Store infrastructure, used solely for the features described in Sections 5 and 6.

The App is distributed through the Apple App Store, and Apple's own store and device services are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. If you have opted in to sharing diagnostics with app developers in your iOS settings, Apple may make aggregate, non-identifying crash or usage information available to developers; we do not embed any analytics SDK of our own and do not receive your practice data through that channel.

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect legal rights, but because the App has no account system and no server-side copy of your data, there is no user database for us to disclose from.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local data. Deleting the App from your device removes its locally stored achievements and preferences.
  • iCloud data. You control the synced record through your Apple ID. To remove it, delete the App's iCloud data in iOS Settings (Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Account Storage). Turning off iCloud Sync alone does not erase a record already stored in iCloud.
  • Purchases. Transaction records are retained by Apple in accordance with its own policies; we cannot delete them.
  • Support messages. If you email us, we use that message only to answer your question and retain it only as long as needed for that purpose.

The App does not offer an in-app "reset all data" control. You may also email us to ask what categories of data the App stores or to request assistance with deletion.

9. Security

Data stored on your device is protected by iOS app sandboxing. When iCloud Sync is enabled, data in transit and at rest is protected by Apple's iCloud security. We apply reasonable measures in the design of the App, but no method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the App requires no account, a parent or guardian who believes a child has stored data through iCloud Sync can remove it using the steps in Section 8.

11. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete personal data. Because the App has no account system and we hold no server-side copy of your data, the most direct way to exercise these rights is through your device and your iCloud settings as described in Section 8. You may also contact us using the details below.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App evolves. Material changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
